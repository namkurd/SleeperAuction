Pick quality by manager

One row per manager, ranked by Overall — a single score (🔥 Banger +2, ✅ Hit +1, 😐 Meh 0, 📉 Bust −1, 💀 Fail −2, averaged over every graded pick) so the best drafting shows up at the top without having to weigh five columns yourself. Click any column header to sort by it instead. Hover (or tap) any number to see the exact picks behind it. Filter to specific seasons or positions below. Kickers, defenses, and picks without a season-finish rank yet aren't counted.
